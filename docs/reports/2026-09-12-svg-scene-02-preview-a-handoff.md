computed gradient paint, font readiness, and non-empty geometry. Noto Sans Latin 400 is the exact

`09aee8065d25508f23a4c3d92cd777ac869c52d93fd868a88f025d888a7937d6`; the template carries its
OFL-1.1 notice and makes no network or host-font assumption.

The tactical fixture remains a projection of already captured disclosed values. Its adapter accepts
no undisclosed source object, and a negative witness proves an identity outside the disclosed visible
set cannot enter selection, focus, or the accessible projection. This work did not access S.I.R.
